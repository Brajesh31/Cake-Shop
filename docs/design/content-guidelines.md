# ✍️ Content and editorial guidelines

## Voice
Use clear, warm, specific language appropriate to a bakery. Avoid exaggerated claims, generic filler, or promises that the business cannot fulfill.

## Product content
A real product record should include:
- Product name and unique identifier
- Accurate description and ingredients where relevant
- Allergen information reviewed by the business
- Current price and currency
- Available sizes or variants
- Availability and lead time
- Product image and alternative text
- Storage or delivery notes where applicable

Do not invent ingredients, allergen guarantees, prices, dietary suitability, or availability.

## Blog content
Each article should have a unique title, author or responsible publisher, publication date, summary, main image, meaningful alt text, and a stable destination. Pagination must reflect actual content rather than placeholder anchors.

## Business information
Confirm address, opening hours, phone, email, delivery areas, and social accounts with the business owner before publishing. Sample content is not proof of a real business detail.

## SEO content
Use a descriptive page title and meta description for each route. Keep one primary page heading, use meaningful internal links, and avoid keyword stuffing. Do not publish duplicate or misleading structured data.

## Editorial review checklist
- [ ] Facts and claims verified
- [ ] Prices and dates current where applicable
- [ ] Spelling and grammar reviewed
- [ ] Image usage rights confirmed
- [ ] Alt text supplied
- [ ] Links checked
- [ ] Privacy and legal claims reviewed
