# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Cake Shop project welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints. We are committed to a respectful, accessible, and harassment-free environment for everyone who contributes, reviews, or discusses the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Critique ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Explain disagreements with clear reasoning and assume good intent where possible.
- Accept constructive feedback and focus on what helps the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information or private communications.
- Keep discussion relevant and accessible to people with different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, threats, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Repeatedly derailing discussion or using demeaning language is also not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, code reviews, and other spaces when someone is representing or participating in the Cake Shop project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this policy to the project maintainer, Brajesh Kumar (@brajesh31), through the contact options on the GitHub profile at https://github.com/brajesh31. Include links or enough context to identify the incident. Please do not post private or sensitive details in a public issue.

The maintainer will review reports as promptly and fairly as possible, respect reporter privacy to the extent practical, and may ask for additional context. Depending on severity and context, actions may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or exclusion from project spaces. The maintainer will communicate the outcome when appropriate.
